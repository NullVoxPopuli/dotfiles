## When writing prose

- avoid LLM spew
- avoid all the banned words and phrases and formatting
- do not repeat yourself
- do not explain things that were explained elsewhere in the document
- do not provide implementation details about a problem when not asked -- high level is fine. humans can figure out implementation on their own.
- adhere to the adhd-friendly-writing skill
- adhere to the tldr writing skill

- always: be precise with word choice
- always: be concise in sentence structure and length
